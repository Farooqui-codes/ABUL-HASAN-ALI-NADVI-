About School

جامعہ ابوالحسن علی ندوی (ربانی فاؤنڈیشن مارول)
HERITAGE OF WISDOM

Named After a Luminary of Islamic Thought

Operating under Rabbani Foundation Marul, Jamia Abul Hasan Ali Nadwi is dedicated to providing high-quality foundational Islamic education and contemporary primary schooling for young boys.

Our academy currently caters from Nursery, LKG, UKG up to 4th Standard. We lay a strong bedrock of Noorani Qaida, Tajweed, basic Arabic, English phonics, mental math, and Sunnah etiquette. Higher grade levels (5th to 10th Standard) are scheduled for gradual expansion in future development phases.

Our Vision

To produce intellectually formidable, spiritually grounded Muslim youth who lead with upright moral integrity, excel at top universities, and serve humanity in accordance with divine prophetic guidance.

Our Mission

To deliver an uncompromisingly high standard of academic education harmonized with thorough memorization and understanding of the Holy Quran, Sunnah manners, Arabic fluency, and modern technical competence.
