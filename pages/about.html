ABOUT OUR INSTITUTION

About School & Philosophy

جامعہ ابوالحسن علی ندوی (ربانی فاؤنڈیشن مارول)
HERITAGE OF WISDOM

Rooted in Sacred Faith, Committed to Academic Excellence

Operating under Rabbani Foundation Marul, Jamia Abul Hasan Ali Nadwi was established to provide early childhood and primary boys with a holistic Islamic education paired with strong academic fundamentals.

Our campus currently focuses on Nursery, LKG, UKG up to 4th Standard. We believe the early years form the cornerstone of human life. We patiently cultivate correct Quranic pronunciation (Makharij & Tajweed), foundational Arabic, fluent English phonics, mental mathematics, and Sunnah morals. Grade 5th to 10th are scheduled in our Phase 2 development.

Nursery to 4th Standard Active Under Rabbani Foundation Marul Dedicated Boys Primary Campus

Quranic Literacy First

Every student learns Noorani Qaida with Tajweed rules and memorizes daily Masnoon Duas and short Surahs with proper articulation.

Primary Academics

Structured English phonics, communicative confidence, foundational mathematics, and nature-inspired environmental studies.

Sunnah & Tarbiyah

Gentle character formation, obedience to parents, truthfulness, cleanliness, and respect for teachers in accordance with the Prophetic example.
