Campus Facilities

EARLY READING CULTURE

Junior Islamic Library & Reading Corner

Carefully curated for junior boys (Nursery to 4th Standard), our reading corner features illustrated Sirah stories, color-coded Noorani Qaida primers, Arabic picture dictionaries, and engaging English phonics storybooks.

  • Child-safe low shelving and quiet wooden reading tables.
  • Weekly storytelling circle bringing Prophetic virtues to life.
  • Book lending recorded digitally via our school management ERP.
SPIRITUAL SANCTUARY

Campus Musalla & Daily Prayer Area

Spiritual nurturing starts from childhood. Our clean campus Musalla allows young boys to learn practical Wudhu manners, align rows (Taswiyat-us-Sufoof), and perform Zuhr Salah in congregation under the loving guidance of their Asatizah.

  • Dedicated, clean ablution station tailored for young students.
  • Daily Sunnah Dua practice circles after congregational prayers.
  • Instilling reverence and discipline for the House of Allah.
MULTIMEDIA LEARNING

Activity & Computer Hall

Equipped with safe, supervised educational multimedia tools to help children visualize phonics sounds, listen to authentic Qari recitations, and master basic keyboard and mouse orientation.

  • Audio-visual Tajweed pronunciation aids with headphones.
  • Child-safe supervised educational mathematics software.
  • Interactive smart screen for classroom storytelling sessions.
SAFE COMMUTE

Supervised Campus Transport

Rabbani Foundation operates safe, dedicated school vans serving Marul and neighboring residential areas, ensuring stress-free pickup and drop-off for our young students.

  • Verified drivers and attentive adult bus conductors.
  • Pre-planned routes covering Marul Central, Colony, and Main Road.
  • Safety protocols ensuring children are handed directly to guardians.
