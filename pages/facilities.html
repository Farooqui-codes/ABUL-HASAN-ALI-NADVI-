INFRASTRUCTURE & CAMPUS

Campus Facilities

سہولیاتِ کیمپس — تعلیمی و تربیتی ماحول
CENTRAL RESEARCH FACILITY

The Maktabah (Central Library)

Named after Hazrat Maulana Syed Abul Hasan Ali Nadwi, our library houses an exceptional collection of over 15,000 volumes across Quranic Tafseer, Hadith encyclopedias, Arabic grammar treatises, world history, mathematics, and contemporary science literature.

  • Quiet reading halls with wooden Quran reading stands (Rehals).
  • Digital cataloging integrated into the ERP library module.
  • High-speed research terminals for senior students and faculty.
SPIRITUAL HEART OF CAMPUS

Grand Musalla & Prayer Hall

Spiritual development is the core of our institution. The centrally located Musalla accommodates 1,000 worshippers, fostering discipline through five daily congregational prayers led by Huffaz scholars.

  • Acoustically designed mihrab and dome architecture.
  • Dedicated ablution (Wudu) areas with clean, continuous water supply.
  • Daily evening spiritual reminder (Tazkeer) circles.
SCIENTIFIC EXPLORATION

STEM & Computer Laboratories

Modern physics, chemistry, biology, and IT laboratories allow our boys to combine deep faith with rigorous empirical science. Safe workstations, digital microscopes, and high-spec desktop computers facilitate practical learning.

  • Standardized safety apparatus and fume ventilation.
  • Python programming, web basics, and digital office productivity suites.
  • Annual inter-school science and innovation exhibition.
SAFE COMMUTE

School Transport Fleet

A dedicated fleet of well-maintained school buses and commuter vans traversing across major city routes, supervised by experienced male drivers and staff with verified records.

  • Controlled routes with designated pickup and drop points.
  • Emergency first-aid kits on every vehicle.
  • Tracked under the ERP Transport Management Module.
