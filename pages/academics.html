THREE PILLARS OF LEARNING

Integrated Academic Tracks

Structured for deep comprehension, scientific curiosity, and spiritual illumination.

Pre-Primary (Nursery, LKG, UKG)

Play-based early learning nurturing young boys with Arabic alphabet recognition, English phonics, numerical counting, and daily Islamic etiquette with caring male teachers.

  • Arabic & English Phonics Foundation
  • Fine motor skills & coloring activity
  • Basic Masnoon Duas & Kalimahs

Primary School (Class 1 to 4)

Comprehensive English-medium primary curriculum covering Mathematics, Environmental Science (EVS), English Language & Grammar, Hindi, and Urdu literature.

  • Solid mental arithmetic & basic geometry
  • English reading fluency & creative writing
  • Science curiosity & visual projects

Noorani Qaida & Nazira

Structured step-by-step Quranic reading with authentic Makharij and Tajweed rules. Students memorize the last 10 Surahs and essential daily supplications.

  • Individualized pacing under expert Qaris
  • Fluent recitation of Para 30 (Juz Amma)
  • Practical Salah (prayer) training
Academic Scope: Jamia Abul Hasan Ali Nadwi currently runs Nursery to 4th Standard. Secondary grade levels (Class 5th through 10th) will be gradually upgraded in upcoming academic phases as students advance.
