Academic Curriculum

شعبہ جاتِ تعلیم — نورانی قاعدہ، تجوید و عصری نصاب
NURSERY TO 4TH STANDARD

Integrated Foundational Curriculum

Structured for deep Quranic comprehension, phonetic language mastery, and mathematical curiosity.

Pre-Primary (Nursery, LKG, UKG)

Play-based early learning nurturing young boys with Arabic alphabet recognition, English phonics, numerical counting, and daily Islamic etiquette with caring male teachers.

  • Arabic & English Phonics Foundation
  • Fine motor skills & coloring activity
  • Basic Masnoon Duas & Kalimahs

Primary School (Class 1 to 4)

Comprehensive English-medium primary curriculum covering Mathematics, Environmental Science (EVS), English Language & Grammar, Hindi, and Urdu literature.

  • Solid mental arithmetic & basic geometry
  • English reading fluency & creative writing
  • Science curiosity & visual projects

Quranic Nazira & Tajweed

Specialized Quranic department where every boy completes Noorani Qaida with precise Makharij (points of articulation), Tajweed rules, and memorizes the 30th Juz.

  • Certified Qari supervision & personal attention
  • Memorization of daily life Sunnah Duas
  • Respectful, encouraging learning atmosphere
