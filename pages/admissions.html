PROCEDURE

4-Step Admission Pathway

Under Rabbani Foundation Marul, admissions for young male children from Nursery, LKG, UKG and Classes 1 to 4 are currently open for academic session 2026–27. Secondary grades (5th–10th) will be introduced in future development phases.

1
Submit Application / Online Enquiry

Fill out the official online form with student and father/guardian details.

2
Friendly Child Interaction & Baseline Check

Gentle assessment in age-appropriate letters, Noorani Qaida phonics, and basic numbers.

3
Parent Consultation

Brief meeting with Headmaster regarding school values, Tarbiyah partnership, and transport.

4
Confirmation & ERP Portal Access

Issuance of enrollment token, book kit, and parent dashboard login credentials.

Required Documents:
  • Student Birth Certificate (or official municipal age proof).
  • Previous School Report Card or Transfer Certificate (for Class 1 to 4).
  • 3 Passport-sized photographs of the male student.
  • Aadhaar / National ID copy of Father or Guardian.
