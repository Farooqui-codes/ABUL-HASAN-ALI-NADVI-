PROCEDURE

4-Step Admission Pathway (Nursery – 4th)

Under Rabbani Foundation Marul, admissions for young male children from Nursery, LKG, UKG and Classes 1 to 4 are open for session 2026–27. Secondary grades (5th–10th) will be introduced in future phases.

1
Submit Application / Online Enquiry

Fill out the demo application form with student and father/guardian details.

2
Written Assessment Test

Evaluation in Basic Mathematics, English, Urdu, and Quran Nazira recitation.

3
Parent & Student Interview

Discussion with the Principal and Academic Committee regarding goals and Tarbiyah.

4
Confirmation & Portal Enrollment

Submission of transfer certificate, ID issuance, and access to student/parent ERP portals.

Mandatory Documents:
  • Birth Certificate / Official Age Verification.
  • Previous School Transfer Certificate (T.C.) & Marks Record.
  • Recent passport-sized photographs of the male student.
  • Copy of Father / Guardian's National Identity Card.
