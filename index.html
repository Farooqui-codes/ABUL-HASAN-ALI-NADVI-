OUR ACADEMIC CURRICULUM

The Nadwi Educational Philosophy

A balanced curriculum integrating sacred traditional Islamic sciences with modern curriculum prescribed for secondary and higher secondary boards.

Noorani Qaida & Nazira

Correct pronunciation (Makharij) and Tajweed rules taught patiently from Nursery and KG through Class 4, instilling deep love for the Quran.

Learn More

English & Phonics

Modern phonetic reading, vocabulary enrichment, spelling bees, and confident conversational skills for junior grades.

Learn More

Primary Math & EVS

Mental arithmetic, basic geometry, science curiosity, and Environmental Studies (EVS) with engaging visual activity books.

Learn More

Daily Sunnah & Adab

Masnoon Duas, manners of eating, greeting with Salam, obedience to parents, and cultivating gentle, upright Islamic character.
