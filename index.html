OUR ACADEMIC CURRICULUM

The Nadwi Educational Philosophy

A balanced foundational curriculum harmonizing sacred Quranic sciences with modern primary education prescribed for junior learners.

Noorani Qaida & Nazira

Correct pronunciation (Makharij) and Tajweed rules taught patiently from Nursery and KG through Class 4, instilling deep love for the Holy Quran.

Learn More

English & Phonics

Phonetic sound recognition, structured reading habits, vocabulary expansion, neat handwriting, and conversational confidence.

Learn More

Primary Math & EVS

Mental arithmetic, basic geometry, curiosity-driven science, and Environmental Studies (EVS) with engaging visual activity books.

Learn More

Daily Sunnah & Adab

Daily Masnoon Duas, table manners, greeting with Salam, kindness to elders, truthfulness, and cultivating righteous Islamic character.

CAMPUS FACILITIES

Enriching Learning Environment

Dedicated, safe facilities purposefully designed for junior boys' early childhood and primary development.

Junior Reading Library

Age-appropriate stories of the Prophets, illustrated moral readers, Noorani Qaida study aids, and junior Arabic picture dictionaries.

Campus Musalla

Calm prayer sanctuary where students practice daily congregational Wudhu, learn prayer postures, and recite daily Sunnah Duas.

Activity & Computer Hall

Interactive phonics listening stations, safe basic computer orientation, and visual arithmetic kits for early grade exploration.
